Submit your animation

Made something yourself? Send it here. Everything gets watched by a person before it goes up, so give it a few days.

What we can accept
  • Original animation that you made, or that you have the creator's permission to publish here.
  • Anything goes for length. A twenty second loop is fine.
  • No AMVs, edits, compilations, reuploads, or clips taken from existing shows. Those get rejected, however good they are.
  • No music you do not have the rights to. That includes the soundtrack.

You need an account to submit, so we have a way to reach you about your work.

However you want to be credited on the site.
A direct link to the video file. archive.org works well and is free. Not a YouTube or Drive page link, the file itself.
Optional. Portrait works best. We will use a placeholder if you skip it.
Shown on the page. A couple of sentences is plenty.
Optional. Email, Discord, wherever you would rather be reached than your account email.
This is my own work, or I have permission from the person who made it. I am allowing Onyx to host and stream it, and I can ask for it to be taken down at any time.
